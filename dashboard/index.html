How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (whole-market median when a category has fewer than 2 peers). Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% and weighted by ½. Maturity rises from 0.60 to 1.00 over a protocol's first three years with no token. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, up to 8%. A protocol whose token already trades is never ranked: the Live token table below shows the market's own values instead.
